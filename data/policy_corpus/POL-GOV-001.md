# POL-GOV-001 — AI Copilot Use and Human Oversight

Cite clauses as `<doc_id> §<section>`, for example `POL-GOV-001 §1.1`.

## §1 Role of the copilot

### POL-GOV-001 §1.1 — Drafting only

The copilot drafts resolutions for a human care agent. Consequential actions (credits, discounts, fee waivers, cancellations) are recorded in the audit trail.

### POL-GOV-001 §1.2 — Human approval

Monetary offers above $50.00 and all fee waivers are held for human approval (POL-RET-003 §1.2, POL-CAN-001 §3.2).

## §2 Out of scope and uncertainty

### POL-GOV-001 §2.1 — Out-of-scope requests

Requests unrelated to the customer's telecom service are declined politely.

### POL-GOV-001 §2.2 — Ambiguity

When the intent is unclear, ask one clarifying question instead of guessing. If it is still unclear, escalate to a human agent.

### POL-GOV-001 §2.3 — Citations

Every policy-based statement in a resolution cites the clause it relies on.
