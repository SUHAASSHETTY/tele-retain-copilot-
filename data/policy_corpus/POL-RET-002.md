# POL-RET-002 — Discount Limits by Plan Tier

Cite clauses as `<doc_id> §<section>`, for example `POL-RET-002 §1.1`.

## §1 Maximum discount percentage

### POL-RET-002 §1.1 — Entry tier

Plans PRE-BASIC, POST-ESSENTIAL: maximum loyalty discount 10% of the monthly plan price.

### POL-RET-002 §1.2 — Mid tier

Plans PRE-PLUS, POST-PREMIUM: maximum loyalty discount 15% of the monthly plan price.

### POL-RET-002 §1.3 — Premium tier

Plans PRE-MAX, POST-UNLIMITED: maximum loyalty discount 20% of the monthly plan price.

## §2 Duration and value

### POL-RET-002 §2.1 — Maximum duration

A loyalty discount may run for at most 6 consecutive billing months.

### POL-RET-002 §2.2 — Offer value

The value of a discount offer is: discount % x monthly plan price x number of months. Taxes and regulatory fees are never discounted. The offer value is compared against the approval threshold in POL-RET-003 §1.

### POL-RET-002 §2.3 — Absolute ceiling

No discount may exceed 25% under any circumstances, including with human approval. Requests above this are refused (POL-RET-004 §1.1).
