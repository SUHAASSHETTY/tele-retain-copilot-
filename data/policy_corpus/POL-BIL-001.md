# POL-BIL-001 — Billing Dispute Rules

Cite clauses as `<doc_id> §<section>`, for example `POL-BIL-001 §1.1`.

## §1 Raising a dispute

### POL-BIL-001 §1.1 — Dispute window

A charge may be disputed within 90 days of the invoice issue_date. Older charges are not eligible for dispute.

### POL-BIL-001 §1.2 — Evidence

The disputed line item must be identified on the customer's own invoice. Duplicate charges (the same item and amount billed more than once in one period) are verifiable directly from the invoice line items.

## §2 Resolving a dispute

### POL-BIL-001 §2.1 — Verified duplicate charges

A verified duplicate charge is credited in full. The credit counts toward the approval threshold in POL-RET-003 §1.

### POL-BIL-001 §2.2 — Unverified charges

Charges that cannot be verified from the invoice are opened as a dispute ticket with a resolution SLA of 10 business days; no credit is granted up front.

### POL-BIL-001 §2.3 — Collections hold

The disputed amount is excluded from collections activity while the dispute is open.
