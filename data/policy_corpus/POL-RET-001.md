# POL-RET-001 — Retention Offer Eligibility

Cite clauses as `<doc_id> §<section>`, for example `POL-RET-001 §1.1`.

## §1 Who may receive a retention offer

### POL-RET-001 §1.1 — Tenure requirement

A monetary retention offer (discount, credit or plan upgrade) may only be made to a customer with a tenure of at least 6 months. Customers below this tenure may receive non-monetary offers only (OFF-DATA-BOOST).

### POL-RET-001 §1.2 — Churn-risk requirement

Customers with churn_risk_label `high` may receive up to the full tier maximum in POL-RET-002 §1. Customers labelled `medium` may receive at most half of the tier maximum, rounded down to a whole percent. Customers labelled `low` may receive non-monetary offers only.

### POL-RET-001 §1.3 — Explicit cancellation intent

When a customer explicitly states they intend to cancel, treat their churn risk as at least `medium` for the purpose of offer eligibility, even if the stored label is `low`.

## §2 Exclusions

### POL-RET-001 §2.1 — Offer cooldown

A customer who received a monetary retention offer in the last 12 months (last_retention_offer_date) is not eligible for another monetary offer. Non-monetary offers remain available.

### POL-RET-001 §2.2 — Fraud and collections

Accounts with fraud_flag set, or with 3 or more late payments in the last 12 months, are not eligible for any retention offer. The contact must be escalated to a human agent.

### POL-RET-001 §2.3 — One offer per contact

At most one monetary retention offer may be presented per contact. Offers may not be stacked (see POL-RET-004 §1.3).
