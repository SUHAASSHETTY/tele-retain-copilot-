# POL-RET-003 — Service Credits and Approval Thresholds

Cite clauses as `<doc_id> §<section>`, for example `POL-RET-003 §1.1`.

## §1 Approval thresholds

### POL-RET-003 §1.1 — Auto-approval limit

A credit or discount whose total value is at or below $50.00 may be granted by the copilot-assisted agent without further approval, provided all eligibility rules are met.

### POL-RET-003 §1.2 — Human approval required

Any credit or discount whose total value is above $50.00 MUST be routed to a human approver (team lead) and MUST NOT be granted automatically. The customer is told the offer is pending approval.

### POL-RET-003 §1.3 — Hard cap

No single credit may exceed $150.00. Values above $150.00 are prohibited and must not be offered, even for approval; escalate the contact to a human agent instead.

## §2 Service credits

### POL-RET-003 §2.1 — Outage credits

A verified service outage longer than 24 hours qualifies for a pro-rated credit of the monthly plan price for each full day affected.

### POL-RET-003 §2.2 — Goodwill credits

Goodwill credits for poor service experience are limited to $50.00 per 12 months per account and count toward the approval threshold above.
