# POL-PLN-001 — Plan Changes

Cite clauses as `<doc_id> §<section>`, for example `POL-PLN-001 §1.1`.

## §1 Upgrades and downgrades

### POL-PLN-001 §1.1 — Upgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately; the new price is pro-rated for the current cycle.

### POL-PLN-001 §1.2 — Downgrades

Downgrades take effect from the next billing cycle.

### POL-PLN-001 §1.3 — Downgrades in contract

Postpaid downgrades before contract_end_date are allowed only to another postpaid plan and reset nothing; the contract end date is unchanged.

## §2 Switching plan type

### POL-PLN-001 §2.1 — Prepaid to postpaid

Moving from prepaid to postpaid requires a credit check and must be completed by a human agent.

### POL-PLN-001 §2.2 — Recommending a plan

Recommend the lowest-priced plan whose data cap covers the customer's 3-month average usage with at least 10% headroom.
