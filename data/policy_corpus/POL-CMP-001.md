# POL-CMP-001 — Complaint Handling and Escalation SLAs

Cite clauses as `<doc_id> §<section>`, for example `POL-CMP-001 §1.1`.

## §1 Severity and SLAs

### POL-CMP-001 §1.1 — Severity levels and targets

| Severity | Definition | Acknowledge within | Resolve within |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Total loss of service or safety issue | 4 hours | 48 hours |
| P2 | Degraded service, repeated billing error | 24 hours | 5 business days |
| P3 | General dissatisfaction, information request | 24 hours | 10 business days |

## §2 Escalation

### POL-CMP-001 §2.1 — Repeat complaints

A customer with 3 or more complaints in 90 days (including the current one) must be escalated to the Complaints Resolution team.

### POL-CMP-001 §2.2 — Regulator or legal mention

If the customer mentions a regulator, ombudsman or legal action, escalate immediately to a human agent.

### POL-CMP-001 §2.3 — No conditional remedies

Remedies must never be conditional on withdrawing the complaint (POL-RET-004 §1.4).
